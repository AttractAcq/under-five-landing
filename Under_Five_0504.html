Questions

Questions you’re about to ask.

Is this a bot?

No. AI writes the first draft, a person reads it, edits it and sends it. Every message that reaches your lead was approved by a human before it went out.

What happens after 21:00?

Overnight leads get an instant acknowledgement so they know they have been heard, and a human reply by 07:05. In practice, almost nobody wants a phone call at 02:00.

Will you know my business well enough?

That is what the onboarding is for, and it is why there is a setup fee. We build a profile of your pricing, your service area, your qualifying questions and your common objections before we answer a single lead. It gets sharper every month.

Do you close the deal?

No. We answer, qualify and book. You close. We will tell you honestly how many appointments we booked — what happens in the room is yours.

What if my leads are poor quality?

You will find out quickly, because we report contact rates per source. Some clients cut spend on a channel within the first month of seeing it.

Can I cancel?

Three months to start, because we staff a seven-day shift for you. After that, 30 days’ notice.
